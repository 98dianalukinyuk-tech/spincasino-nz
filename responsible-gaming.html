Set the boundary before the evening begins

Gambling is entertainment with uncertain outcomes and real financial risk. A win is never guaranteed.

Choose a budget you can afford, decide how long you will stay and do not chase losses. Take a break when the plan changes or the experience stops feeling enjoyable.
